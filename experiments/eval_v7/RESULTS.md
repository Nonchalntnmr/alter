## Limits

- **Models throughout.** The people, the task writer and the judges are models of the same family as the system under test.
- **Sample.** n = 24 pairs, one run each. Non-significance is not equivalence.
- **Enrichment.** Half the pool needs a question by design, which is not real-world frequency.
- **Labels.** The fact labels (essential, discoverable, volunteered) are the writer's.
- **G is more than one change.** G differs from A in the Third Eye brief, the goal instructions, verification prompts and the goal loop, so a difference cannot be split between them.
- **Corrections differ in form.** A continues its conversation; G starts a follow-up goal carrying the request and the correction.
